# Sample article (for the Study Hub Evidence Notebook)

**Title:** How many times per week should you train?

A ready-to-paste article you can recreate in the app under **My Articles** (create
an article → add each claim → link it to the study, picking the relationship).
First save the three studies below to your Library from search (e.g. search the
titles / PMIDs), then link them to the claims.

---

## Article content (paste into the "Article title" + content box)

> **Title:** How many times per week should you train?
>
> The "right" training frequency is one of the most-asked questions in lifting.
> The short version, from the meta-analyses I've read: train each major muscle
> group **at least twice a week** — once a week leaves gains on the table — and
> treat **three times a week** as a strong, recoverable upper bound. Beyond
> roughly twice weekly the data show diminishing returns, so add frequency only
> if it fits your recovery, not for its own sake.

## Claims (add each as a claim and link the study)

1. **"Training each muscle group twice per week produces significantly greater
   hypertrophy than once per week."**
   - Linked study: **PMID 30558493** — *How many times per week should a muscle
     be trained to maximize muscle hypertrophy? A systematic review and
     meta-analysis* (Schoenfeld et al., 2019).
   - Relationship: **supports**

2. **"On a volume-equated basis, twice-weekly training of a muscle group beats
   once-weekly for growth, so once a week is a poor default for hypertrophy."**
   - Linked study: **PMID 27102172** — *Effects of Resistance Training Frequency
     on Measures of Muscle Hypertrophy: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis*
     (Schoenfeld et al., 2016).
   - Relationship: **supports**

3. **"For strength, training a muscle group two to three times per week is
   superior to once per week."**
   - Linked study: **PMID 29470825** — *Effect of Resistance Training Frequency
     on Gains in Muscular Strength: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis*
     (Schoenfeld et al., 2019).
   - Relationship: **supports**

4. **"Three times per week per muscle is a sensible upper bound, but the
   evidence shows diminishing returns beyond about twice weekly — so 3× is
   optional and depends on recovery, not a clear requirement."**
   - Linked study: **PMID 30558493** (same as claim 1).
   - Relationship: **contextual** (the same meta-analysis that supports 2×>1×
     finds no clear added benefit beyond 2×; this reflects my preference for 3×).

## Why these relationships are honest

The literature is clear that **2× beats 1×** for both hypertrophy (27102172,
30558493) and strength (29470825) — so claims 1–3 are straightforwardly
"supports". Claim 4 encodes your belief that **3× is best**: the studies support
2× as the reliable minimum, but the "3× is best" part is a **contextual**
interpretation (my recovery/training preference), not something the meta-analyses
prove over 2×. Marking it "contextual" keeps the notebook defensible — the "check
alignment" tool should flag claim 1 as aligned and keep claim 4 flagged as
interpretation rather than a strict fact.
